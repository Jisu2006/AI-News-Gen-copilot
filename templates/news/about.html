About AI-NewsGen Copilot

AI-NewsGen Copilot is an AI-powered digital newspaper platform designed to help users submit news information and generate structured news drafts using artificial intelligence.

The platform combines user-submitted information, AI-assisted news drafting, and administrator review to create a controlled digital news publishing workflow.

How It Works

  • User submits information about a news incident.
  • AI generates a structured news draft.
  • The generated draft is sent to the administrator for review.
  • The administrator can edit, approve, request information, or reject the news.
  • Only approved news can be published.

Key Features

  • AI-assisted news generation
  • User news submission
  • Administrator review and approval
  • Breaking and featured news
  • News search and filtering
  • Category, location and date filtering
  • Image and video support
  • Public digital newspaper

Purpose

The main purpose of AI-NewsGen Copilot is to simplify the process of converting user-provided incident information into a readable and structured news draft, while keeping administrator review as an important step before publication.
